Our approach

1

Mapping & audit

Assessment of your exposure (systems, network, applications) and prioritization of vulnerabilities based on their real-world criticality.

2

Implementation of recommendations: network segmentation, access management, security policies and a prioritized remediation plan.

24/7 SOC monitoring of your systems, with detection, qualification and escalation of alerts by our analysts.

Rapid intervention in the event of a confirmed incident, containment, forensic analysis and post-crisis debrief.
